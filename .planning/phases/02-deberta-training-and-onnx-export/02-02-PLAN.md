<objective>
Execute 2-3 rounds of adversarial data augmentation (PWWS) + FreeLB retraining to produce a hardened v4 MiniLM model with reduced attack success rate. Update fetch_model.py for v4 artifact.

Purpose: This plan transforms the tooling from Plan 01 into a hardened model by running the iterative augmentation-retrain cycle. Each round generates PWWS adversarial examples against the current model, merges them into the training set, and retrains with FreeLB. ASR is measured between rounds to track improvement and decide when to stop.

Output: Hardened v4 dual-output ONNX model (logits + cls_embedding), per-round ASR measurements, augmented training datasets with provenance, updated fetch_model.py.
</objective>
